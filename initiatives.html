Grassroots Leadership

Community & Impact

Bridging the gap between clinical research and community health through inclusive education, adolescent empowerment, and social cohesion initiatives in Syria.

Ro'ya

As Scientific Team Leader, I develop health education materials adapted for individuals with visual and hearing impairments — first-aid principles translated into formats that don't assume a learner can see a diagram or hear an instruction. The team has trained over 200 participants. This role taught me that "accessible" isn't a simplified version of standard content; it's a redesign built around a different starting point entirely.

Naseej (Caritas Syria)

I coordinate health education activities within Naseej, a social cohesion initiative bringing together parents, teachers, and children in post-conflict communities. The goal isn't just health knowledge transfer — it's rebuilding the trust and routine that make people receptive to that knowledge in the first place.

Watad (Caritas Syria)

Through Watad, I design and supervise adolescent health education programs. Adolescents are an age group public health strategy often skips past — too old for pediatric programming, too young to be reached through adult channels — and this initiative exists to close that gap.

Background

Who I Am

I grew up in Syria during years of conflict, which shaped everything about how I think about health. I learned early that illness does not distribute itself equally — it follows the lines of poverty, displacement, and exclusion. That understanding became the foundation of everything I have done since.

I am completing my medical degree at Damascus University, graduating with a 91% average. But the most important education has happened outside the classroom — building research networks, designing community health programmes, and asking questions that clinical medicine alone cannot answer.

My work sits at the intersection of three things I refuse to keep separate: generating evidence, making it accessible, and reaching the people it is meant to serve.

Community & Impact

Inclusive Health & Leadership Initiatives

Bridging academic medicine with grassroots action to empower underserved groups, build research capacity, and improve community health literacy.

Co-Founder & Scientific Team Leader

Ro'ya Initiative — Inclusive First Aid Education

A pioneer health initiative dedicated to making lifesaving first-aid knowledge accessible to individuals with visual and hearing impairments.

  • Designed customized, tactile, and sensory-adapted first aid educational kits for participants.
  • Trained over 200 participants with disability-inclusive pedagogical methodologies.
  • Promoted equal access to emergency preparedness and health education across local communities.
Founder & Director

MedRAN (Medical Research & Academic Network)

An academic mentorship and capacity-building network designed to guide medical students and early-career researchers through evidence-based research methodologies.

  • Developed and led a comprehensive 7-session core research methodology curriculum.
  • Mentored medical students in study design, data collection, statistical analysis, and manuscript preparation.
  • Fostered a collaborative digital ecosystem for multi-center undergraduate epidemiological research.
Community Mobilizer & Educator

Social Cohesion & Public Health Advocacy

Active engagement in community dialogue, post-conflict social cohesion programs, and public health awareness lectures.

  • Facilitated community mobilization and dialogue sessions within Caritas Syria's Social Cohesion program.
  • Delivered targeted public health awareness presentations, including chronic disease and diabetes management sessions.
  • Advocated for preventive medicine and health equity in underserved regions across Syria.

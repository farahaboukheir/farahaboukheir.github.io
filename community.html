Community & Impact

Inclusive Health, Peacebuilding & Social Impact

Bridging medical knowledge with grassroots community empowerment, peacebuilding, and inclusive health education across Syrian communities.

Co-Founder & Scientific Team Leader

Ro'ya Initiative (مبادرة رؤيا) — Pioneer Inclusive First Aid

The first medical team in Syria dedicated to providing specialized first-aid and emergency preparedness education for visually impaired, deaf, and mute individuals.

  • Designed customized, tactile, and sensory-adapted first aid educational kits for participants.
  • Trained over 200 participants using tailored disability-inclusive pedagogical methodologies.
  • Promoted health equality and self-reliance in emergency situations among vulnerable populations.

Community Mobilizer & Project Coordinator

Caritas Syria — Peacebuilding & Social Cohesion

Following years of conflict and during a critical period in Syria, organized community dialogue sessions to promote peacebuilding, social cohesion, and resilience. Coordinated two major initiatives supported by Swiss funding:

Watad Initiative (مبادرة وتد)

Rehabilitated the Cultural Center to serve as a safe space for adolescents across 4 specialized clubs: Health & Wellbeing Club, Environmental Club, Debating Club, and Physics Club.

Naseej Initiative (مبادرة نسيج)

Designed social cohesion and peacebuilding sessions targeting the Educational Triangle: Children, Parents, and Teachers to foster a supportive and unified community network.

Community Educator

Towards a Better Future

Organized local community workshops and dialogue sessions aimed at spreading civic culture, health literacy, and youth empowerment in local Syrian communities.

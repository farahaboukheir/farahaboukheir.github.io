Scholarly Work

Research Portfolio

My research investigates health literacy — what people know about their health and why that knowledge often fails to reach them — across contexts ranging from cardiovascular disease to mental health to disability inclusion in Syria.

Knowledge Translation & Capacity Building

Publishing research answers the "what do people know" question, but it doesn't answer "who gets to do the knowing." That gap is what led me to co-found MedRAN (Medical Research and Academic Network) and establish SheResearch, a structured seven-session research methodology curriculum I designed and delivered online to 26 female medical students — teaching the same research skills I'd had to teach myself, without a formal pathway to learn them. I currently mentor five students through their own research projects.

For me, this is the other half of health literacy work: it's not enough to generate evidence about excluded communities. Communities — and the researchers who come from them — need the tools to generate that evidence themselves.

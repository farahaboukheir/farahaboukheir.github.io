Extracurricular Leadership RBCs Team

Medical Content Transcriber & Team Lead — RBCs Team (Mar 2023 – Sep 2025)

RBCs is a volunteer academic team of medical students producing free educational content and running health awareness campaigns for the community, under the philosophy that knowledge — like red blood cells — should keep moving and reaching every part of the body that needs it.

I managed a team of 74 volunteers over one year, coordinating workflow and quality through weekly meetings, and separately led a 12-member subgroup responsible for the pediatrics course content. In 2024, I was ranked the team's top-performing volunteer across all projects and campaigns.

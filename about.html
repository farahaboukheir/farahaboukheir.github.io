My Identity

About Me

I am Farah Abou Kheir, a medical graduate from Damascus University with a dedicated focus on health promotion, epidemiology, and evidence-based healthcare.

Throughout my medical journey, I have been deeply interested in how structured health knowledge influences lives and how modern healthcare must move beyond treating illness toward prevention and population-level health outcomes. This drive encourages me to bridge the gap between rigorous research, clinical practice, and direct community engagement.

My academic and research footprint spans pediatric health, digital health, epidemiological surveys, and disability-inclusive interventions. These experiences have reinforced my conviction that effective health policies require robust evidence tailored to community-specific needs.

Beyond research, I am committed to capacity building and social leadership. I co-founded Roaya Initiative, designed to deliver adapted first-aid training for individuals with visual and hearing impairments, and founded MedRAN (Medical Research and Academic Network) to empower medical students with essential research methodologies.

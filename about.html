My Identity

About Me

I am Farah Abou Kheir, a medical student at Damascus University with a strong interest in health promotion, public health, and evidence-based healthcare.

Throughout my medical journey, I have been interested in how health knowledge can influence people’s lives and how healthcare can move beyond treating illness towards prevention and better health outcomes. This interest has encouraged me to explore the role of education, research, and community engagement in improving health.

I have been involved in research projects focusing on areas such as pediatric health, digital health, women’s health, and disability inclusion. These experiences helped me understand the importance of evidence in designing effective health interventions and addressing the needs of different communities.

My work also includes developing health education initiatives for diverse groups. I co-founded Roaya Team, which focuses on first-aid education for people with visual and hearing impairments, and founded MedRAN (Medical Research and Academic Network) to support medical students in building research skills and engaging with scientific knowledge.

I believe that improving health starts with making reliable information accessible and creating opportunities for people to participate in their own health decisions. I hope to continue developing projects that connect medical knowledge with communities and contribute to more inclusive and preventive healthcare.
